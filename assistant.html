Rocket launcher - Grade 8

Confirmed Tuesday, 10 Oct 2026 - 09:00-09:45 - Lab A

Facilitator: M. Shikongo

Your role: Volunteer assistant - materials and support

Group: 24 learners (Junior secondary)

Duration: 45 minutes

Before learners arrive

Finish this about 10 minutes before the session.

Materials to prepare

  • 6 plastic bottles
  • 3 sheets of cardboard
  • 1 roll masking tape
  • 1 measuring tape (10 m)
  • Scissors (facilitator)
  • 1 printed step sheet per group

If something is missing, note it below.

Running order

  1. 0:00 - 0:05 Safety briefing

    Hold the sample rocket at the launch line.

  2. 0:05 - 0:15 Build fins

    Walk between tables and offer pre-cut templates.

  3. 0:15 - 0:25 Assemble launcher

    Help groups tape the bottle to the fins.

  4. 0:25 - 0:35 Launch and measure

    Hold the measuring tape and call out distances.

  5. 0:35 - 0:45 Discuss fair testing

    Ask groups what they changed between launches.

Safety notes

  • Launch outdoors, away from faces and windows.
  • Keep a 3 m clear zone around the launch line.
  • Only the facilitator cuts bottles or cardboard.
  • Stop if wind makes launches unpredictable.
  • Learners with asthma or mobility aids can record results.

Inclusion prompts

  • Offer pre-cut fin templates to learners with motor difficulties.
  • Allow distances in steps or centimetres.
  • Rotate roles so every learner launches once.
  • Pair a confident reader with a learner who reads slowly.
  • Repeat safety instructions verbally and on the sheet.

Your notes for the facilitator

Only the facilitator can see these. Do not write learner names.
